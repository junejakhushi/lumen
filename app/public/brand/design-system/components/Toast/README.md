# Toast

A short, inverted confirmation that appears near the bottom and leaves on its own.

- **Props:** `tone` `"neutral" | "positive" | "error"` (a small dot, never colour alone), `children` (one line), `action` + `onAction`, `onClose`.
- Inverted (`text` ground, `text-inverse` label) with a gold hairline outline. Errors use `role="alert"`.
- Copy: “Snapshot saved”, “Consultation booked for Tue 22 Sep, 7:30 PM IST”.
