Security, by design

CAD never leaves private storage.

Designers’ files stay in the atelier’s own storage; nothing public points at them.

Only preview meshes are served.

Decimated meshes, behind an access gate with expiring codes.

The camera never leaves the device.

Tracking runs in the browser. No frame is recorded or uploaded.

No AI services anywhere.

Weights and stones come from geometry. No client data goes to a third party.
