Two things slow every sale

One

Clients can’t try pieces that aren’t in the room.

So they hesitate, or book a visit only to find out whether a piece suits them.

Two

Quotes wait on CAD weights.

A price needs the gold weight and the stones. Today a designer opens the CAD file and works it out by hand, days later.

Both problems are about the same moment: the client is interested, and the atelier can’t show or price the piece yet.
